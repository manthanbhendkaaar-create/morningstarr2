Questions

How do I calculate the ROI of automating a task?

Hours saved per month = minutes per task × times per week × 4.33 weeks ÷ 60. Money saved = hours saved × the hourly cost of the person doing it, minus any monthly tool costs. Payback time = the one-off build cost ÷ money saved per month.

Why multiply by 4.33 weeks?

A year has 52 weeks and 12 months, so an average month has 52 ÷ 12 = 4.33 weeks. Using 4 weeks would undercount the time spent by about 8%.

What hourly cost should I use?

Use what an hour of that person's time really costs you: salary or contractor rate plus employer costs, or for your own time, what you could bill for that hour. The calculator works in any currency.

What if the automation only does part of the task?

Set the share automated below 100%. If a person still checks each result for a fifth of the time, enter 80%. The hours and money saved are scaled down to match.

What is a good payback time for an automation?

There is no single rule; it depends on your cash flow. Many businesses are comfortable with builds that pay for themselves within a year. The calculator shows payback in months so you can compare options.

Does this include the time to build and maintain the automation?

Only what you enter. Add the build cost as the one-off cost and any software, AI or hosting fees as the monthly tool cost. Fixes and changes over time are not included unless you add them to the monthly cost.
